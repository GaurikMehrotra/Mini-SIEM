# Dataset admission

At the user's request, a public lab subset was selected and evaluated after reviewing
its license and provenance: Splunk attack_data, Apache-2.0, pinned commit recorded in
configs/public_dataset.json. See [review and results](public_dataset.md). It provides
scenario/ATT&CK labels, not event-level malicious/benign labels; public event precision
and false-positive rates remain unknown. No upstream records are committed. Local-laptop
results cannot be produced on this cloud machine.

Use only synthetic records, a reviewed public labeled source, or your isolated lab. Never
use home/workplace/third-party telemetry. Source terms can restrict redistribution and
commercial use and may contain personal data despite public availability. Do not commit
upstream data. Flow-feature CSV datasets are not automatically interchangeable with raw
Zeek/Suricata logs; without a defensible adapter and matched ground truth, comparing rule
recall would be misleading. Do not fabricate field mappings to fit labels.

For each proposed dataset record: canonical source URL, release/version, checksum, exact
license/terms URL and review date, permissible use, attribution, whether redistribution is
allowed, personal-data assessment, timestamp zone, parsing adapter, label meaning and units,
and evaluation split. The user authorized source selection and review for the recorded Splunk subset. New
sources require their own terms/provenance review. Other candidates may include CIC's
published intrusion-detection datasets at https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ ; no license or
appropriateness is asserted here for those other sources. Read the exact release's
terms rather than relying on a mirror's README.

The reproducible evaluation is fully synthetic. It generates ten target scenarios plus
benign noise from original test definitions, with seed 42 and deterministic source/raw IDs.
Ground truth lists scenario instances, intended rules, source and evidence IDs. Use a fixed
`now` when comparing byte-level output; the CLI defaults to current UTC for retention-safe
demos. Reports state their denominators and exclusions.

Tiny fixtures were generated by original lab/traffic/benign_pcap.py and pinned Zeek/Suricata
8.0.2, plus handcrafted synthetic DNS/TLS/notice/syslog/web/JSON records. All users/hosts are
synthetic and IPs are RFC5737. No PCAP or database is committed. See fixtures/README.md.
